Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

GroScan ("the App") is built around one rule: your grocery and budget data belongs to you, and it stays on your device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

What we collect

GroScan does not collect, transmit, or store your shopping data — item names, prices, trip history, or budget figures — on any server or cloud platform. Everything you enter or scan is saved locally in the App's storage on your iPhone.

Camera access

GroScan requests camera access solely to let you photograph price tags for faster entry. Photos are processed on your device to extract a price and are not uploaded anywhere. You can also use manual entry mode without ever granting camera access.

No accounts, no cloud sync

GroScan does not require you to create an account, sign in, or connect to any online service to use its core features. Because there is no cloud sync, uninstalling the App or losing your device will also remove your locally stored data — we recommend using your device's built-in backup (such as iCloud device backup) if you'd like a copy preserved.

Analytics and third parties

GroScan does not embed third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that collect personal shopping data. Aggregated, anonymous crash diagnostics may be provided by Apple's standard App Store platform tools, governed by Apple's own privacy terms, not ours.

In-app purchases

GroScan Premium subscriptions are processed entirely through Apple's App Store billing system. GroScan does not receive or store your payment details; that information is handled directly by Apple in accordance with the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

GroScan is a household budgeting tool intended for general audiences and is not directed at children. As the App collects no data off-device, no personal information from any user, including children, is transmitted or stored externally.

Support & FAQ

We're happy to help
Does GroScan work without an internet connection?

Yes. Since GroScan stores everything on your device, scanning, manual entry, and budget tracking all work fully offline.

Will my data sync across my devices?

Not currently. Because GroScan doesn't use cloud storage, trip and budget data stays on the device it was entered on.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Local app data is removed when GroScan is deleted, unless it's included in a device-level backup like iCloud. We recommend keeping device backups on if you want to preserve trip history.

How do I cancel GroScan Premium?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, select GroScan, and choose Cancel Subscription.

Can I change the currency GroScan uses?

Yes. Go to GroScan's in-app Settings and select your preferred currency from the list — all totals and budgets will update to match.

The camera isn't reading a price tag correctly. What should I do?

Try scanning again in brighter, even lighting, holding the phone steady and close to the tag. If it still doesn't read correctly, you can always add or correct the item using manual mode.
